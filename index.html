About this app & important disclaimer

Amico Light Tools is a quick-reference field tool for common electrical calculations used in U.S. residential and commercial work. It is built around formulas and reference figures that are widely published and taught throughout the trade — including values commonly associated with the National Electrical Code (NEC / NFPA 70), such as Table 310.16 ampacities, Chapter 9 conduit/conductor fill tables, 314.16(B) box-fill allowances, and Article 430 motor tables.

Amico Light Tools is a reference and estimating aid — not a substitute for the current code book, engineering judgment, or a licensed electrician. The NEC is updated on a three-year cycle (e.g., 2017, 2020, 2023…), and the version actually in force — along with any state or local amendments — depends on what your Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) has adopted. Always verify calculations and sizing decisions against the official, currently adopted code and any local amendments before relying on them for installations, designs, permits, or inspections.

Spotted a value that looks off, or have a calculator you'd like added? Treat the results here as a starting point for your own verification, not a final answer.
